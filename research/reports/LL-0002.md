# LL-0002 — Live control pilot

## Outcome

All 24 planned GPT-5.6 Luna subject runs completed under isolated two-turn
workspaces. There were no harness failures. The run used approximately 996,105
input tokens, including 182,016 cached tokens, and 18,498 output tokens. At the
published GPT-5.6 Luna token rates on the execution date, estimated model cost
was approximately `$0.94`.

The aggregate scorer appeared to favor the incumbent loop:

| Condition | Fixed Brier ↓ | Model accuracy | Decision accuracy | Probe value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| current loop | 0.0006 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0.833 |
| no loop | 0.0278 | 0.833 | 0.833 | 0.833 |
| ritualistic | 0.0452 | 0.833 | 0.833 | 0.833 |
| local optimizer | 0.0563 | 0.833 | 0.833 | 0.833 |

These numbers are not valid evidence that the loop lowers prediction error.

## Material evaluator failure

Every meaningful aggregate difference came from `PW-001`. The selected `P2`
observation ruled out underscore rejection but left stale old-tenant cache and
long-value truncation unresolved. The incumbent transcripts explicitly
acknowledged that unresolved alternative, then assigned checkpoint outcomes
probabilities at or near `0` and `1` matching the hidden mechanism.

The no-loop subjects often assigned `0.5` to the unresolved checkpoint. Against
the one fixed hidden truth, the proper score rewarded the incumbent's lucky
overconfidence and penalized epistemically warranted uncertainty. Two
repetitions are insufficient for proper scoring to recover its expected
incentive.

The contact metric also failed to discriminate. The three probes in `PW-001`
were symmetric under the unstated equal prior, yet the oracle arbitrarily named
`P3` preferred. Across all worlds, every condition converged on nearly the same
probe pattern. `PW-002` and `PW-003` then ceilinged across conditions.

## What the run did establish

- The isolated Codex subject runner works with GPT-5.6 Luna.
- Exact instruction conditions were loaded; the full loop incurred roughly
  1,700 additional input tokens per turn relative to the short controls.
- Structured two-turn evidence, event streams, usage, hashes, and failures can
  be preserved reproducibly.
- The live controls found an evaluator weakness that the synthetic controls did
  not contain.

## Adaptation

`EVAL-0002` replaces forced model choice with posterior distributions, balances
and randomizes hidden mechanisms, derives contact value from explicit priors and
likelihoods, and scores calibration across the allocation. Hypothesis labels and
surface order will be permuted to reduce author-intent leakage. The ceilinged
worlds require harder variants.

No intervention is promoted from `LL-0002`. Its disposition is
`evaluator-repair`.
