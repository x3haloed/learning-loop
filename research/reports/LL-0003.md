# LL-0003 — Balanced live-control challenge

## Outcome

All 48 frozen GPT-5.6 Luna runs completed with zero harness failures. The
allocation contained twelve generated worlds with balanced hidden mechanisms,
randomized presentation and probe mappings, explicit priors, and deterministic
likelihoods. Each of four instruction conditions saw the same worlds once.

The challenge does **not** establish that the current Adaptive Work Loop lowers
prediction error. It does establish that the explicit prediction/contact/update
contract in the task prompt strongly induces the target behavior even when the
standing prose instructs a ritual or a local optimizer.

| Condition | Bayes L1 ↓ | Posterior Brier ↓ | Checkpoint Brier ↓ | Probe ratio ↑ | Decision ↑ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| current loop | 0.0000 | 0.0312 | 0.0590 | 0.9901 | 1.000 |
| no loop | 0.0190 | 0.0268 | 0.0602 | 0.9901 | 1.000 |
| ritualistic | 0.0375 | 0.0395 | 0.0639 | 1.0000 | 1.000 |
| local optimizer | 0.0375 | 0.0395 | 0.0395 | 1.0000 | 1.000 |

No condition dominates these metrics. The current loop most closely reproduced
the exact Bayesian posterior, but no-loop had the lowest posterior Brier and the
local optimizer had the lowest fixed-checkpoint Brier. All conditions scored
1.0 on both decision and next-action accuracy, so those endpoints ceilinged.

## Why this is not evidence of an instruction effect

The primary separation is sparse:

- Current-loop matched the exact posterior on 12/12 worlds (within rounding).
- No-loop had two material posterior deviations; ritualistic and local-optimizer
  each had one, on the same world.
- Current-loop and no-loop each selected one suboptimal probe. The ritualistic
  and local-optimizer controls selected the optimum on all twelve worlds.
- Every condition chose the evidence-relative decision and next action on every
  world, including the control explicitly biased toward ritual continuation.

The task schema required a probe, a numerical prior-predictive forecast, a
posterior, fixed checkpoint predictions, and an evidence-relative action. That
machinery was a more powerful behavioral intervention than the standing prose.
This is the intended kind of “hack,” but it masks whether `AGENTS.md` itself
contributes anything.

## Evaluator audit

EVAL-0002 repaired the fatal fixed-truth/overconfidence problem in EVAL-0001.
Balanced truth and exact Bayesian reference values prevented lucky certainty
from being treated as learning. Its synthetic qualification remains valid.

The live challenge exposed two additional weaknesses:

1. Decision and next-action tasks were too explicit and ceilinged.
2. The evaluator did not score coherence between a reported posterior and the
   checkpoint forecasts implied by that posterior.

Four live responses contained material forecast/posterior inconsistency:
current-loop `CW-002`, no-loop `CW-002` and `CW-012`, and ritualistic `CW-008`.
The mean diagnostic coherence error was 0.0278, 0.0333, 0.0222, and 0.0000 for
current-loop, no-loop, ritualistic, and local-optimizer respectively. This is a
post-hoc evaluator audit, not a retroactively promoted primary metric.

## Cost and operational evidence

The subjects used 2,024,017 input tokens (516,096 cached) and 38,777 output
tokens. At the same published Luna rates used for LL-0002, estimated cost was
approximately `$1.9`, below the `$3` planning ceiling. The current-loop
condition consumed 552,773 input tokens versus roughly 489k–492k for each short
control, without a commensurate endpoint advantage.

## Disposition

`EVAL-0002` is **qualified for balanced posterior/contact measurement but not
for intervention promotion**. LL-0003 is an evaluator-and-contract result, not
an incumbent win.

The next allocation should factor prose and machinery independently:

- standing instruction: current loop vs no loop;
- contract: explicit ledger/schema vs ordinary task prompt;
- scoring: hidden, outcome-grounded forecasts with a coherence check;
- difficulty: observations that require extracting likelihoods from artifacts
  rather than copying explicit probabilities;
- endpoints: non-ceilinged decisions with asymmetric consequences.

That 2×2 design can answer whether machinery causes execution, whether prose
adds anything once machinery exists, and whether either transfers to less
scaffolded contact with reality.
