# LL-0005 — Noisy 2×2 challenge (proposed)

## Status

`planned` / `unexecuted`. This is the research-director selection after
`LL-0004`. Do not treat this document as a result.

## Why this is next

`LL-0003` and `LL-0004` left three live hypotheses:

1. **Current-loop prose actually improves Bayesian updating.** Supported only
   weakly: exact posterior match on both current-loop cells, versus Bayes L1
   `0.0756` for no-loop-plain.
2. **The apparent edge is an artifact of deterministic collapse.** One perfect
   contact often pinned a model at 1.0, so later forecasts and the 0.80 commit
   rule became schema copies.
3. **Task machinery, not `AGENTS.md`, is the actuator.** The instrumented JSON
   contract still forced a probe, a posterior, and a decision.

The program asks for one causal unit. The cheapest unit that can kill (2) and
stress (1) without inventing a new evaluator epoch is **probe noise**.

`worlds-0003` keeps the same 2×2, seed, families, and priors as `LL-0004` and
changes only the observation channel to `P(positive|target)=0.70`,
`P(positive|other)=0.15`. An honest Bayesian on the information-maximizing
probe now:

| Worlds | Max posterior | Honest decision |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 7 / 12 | ≥ 0.80 | commit (`D*` / `N1`) |
| 5 / 12 | < 0.80 | withhold (`D4` / `N2`) |
| CW-004, CW-010 | 0.792 | withhold, 0.008 below the rule |

That is enough residual uncertainty to make overconfidence, under-updating, and
restraint visible. Deterministic `worlds-0002` committed on 9/12 preferred
probes and then ceilinged live decision/next-action at 1.0.

## What we are not doing yet

- **Implicit likelihoods in artifacts.** That would change two units at once
  (noise *and* whether numbers are copyable). If `LL-0005` still shows a
  current-loop Bayes-L1 edge, that is the next challenger.
- **A free-action lane.** Still required by the program, but it answers a
  different question (does prose induce ceremony without a schema). Run it
  after the instrumented noisy worlds either separate or fail to separate.
- **Evaluator replacement.** `EVAL-0002` remains qualified for posterior and
  contact measurement. Decision/next-action stay diagnostics.

## Design

Frozen contract: `research/contracts/challenge-0005.json`.

- Conditions: current-loop × {contract, plain} and no-loop × {contract, plain}.
- Worlds: twelve balanced CHALLENGE variants in `worlds-0003.json`.
- Model: GPT-5.6 Luna, medium reasoning, 48 isolated two-turn runs.
- Planning ceiling: `$3`. `LL-0003`/`LL-0004` cost about `$1.9–$1.95`.
- Skills remain uninstalled.

Primary metrics: posterior entropy gap, Bayes L1, posterior Brier, fixed
checkpoint Brier, probe information ratio.

Diagnostics: preferred probe, probe-prediction Brier, posterior–checkpoint
coherence L1, decision accuracy, next-action accuracy.

## Harness repairs that are not experimental factors

Two defects would have made the contracted 2×2 uninterpretable:

- `score_response` already computed `posterior_entropy_gap`, but the runner
  dropped it from aggregation.
- The plain prompt replacement looked for `Select a model intervention…0.80`
  while every world says `Choose a model intervention…0.80`, so `LL-0004`
  leaked the numeric commit rule into the “plain” cells.

Those are fixed before subject execution. They do not change instruction
packages, model, budget, or hidden truths.

## Consequential prediction

If hypothesis (1) is real, current-loop cells will keep lower Bayes L1 and
entropy gap than no-loop-plain, and will withhold on CW-004 / CW-010 more
often than they commit.

If hypothesis (2) is right, the current-loop Bayes-L1 edge from `LL-0004`
will shrink or reverse once a single observation cannot certify a model.

If hypothesis (3) is right, contract vs plain will move more than loop vs
no-loop, especially on entropy gap and the knife-edge withhold worlds.

A result that again scores 1.0 on decision and next-action, or that again
fails to separate any primary metric, kills this allocation for promotion
and sends the next step to implicit-likelihood worlds or a free-action lane.

## Kill conditions

- More than 25% harness failures.
- Hidden truth or scorer details reach a subject.
- Estimated spend exceeds the planning ceiling before dispatch completes.
- Honest synthetic controls on `worlds-0003` collapse to certainty (already
  falsified locally: max honest posterior stays below 0.999).

## Execution

```bash
python3 -m unittest discover -s research/tests -v
python3 research/bin/run_challenge.py \
  --contract research/contracts/challenge-0005.json \
  --evidence-root research/evidence/challenge/LL-0005
```

Do not reuse the `LL-0003` or `LL-0004` evidence roots.

## Interpretation boundary

A successful run can qualify noisy-channel measurement and can say whether
the `LL-0004` prose edge survived residual uncertainty. It cannot promote
`AGENTS.md`. Promotion still requires adversarial transcript review and a
fresh confirmatory allocation.
